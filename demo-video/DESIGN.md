# DSH iOS Demo Video Identity

## Style Prompt

A precise, restrained developer-tool demo built around the established DSH whale mark. The real product footage remains the hero. Use a near-black technical canvas, clean white typography, and a single muted teal accent derived from the DSH whale reference. Motion should feel responsive and intentional: short confident entrances, quiet holds, and simple focus-pull transitions. The result should read as trustworthy product evidence rather than a concept trailer.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0B0F10`
- Foreground: `#F4F7F5`
- Muted copy: `#B8C4C1`
- DSH teal: `#5B817F`
- Active teal: `#7DD8BE`

## Typography

- English headlines: `Archivo Black`; Chinese display copy: `PingFang SC`; 800 weight
- Labels, timing, and technical metadata: `IBM Plex Mono`, monospace; 400-600 weight

## Motion

- Entrances: 0.25-0.65 seconds with `expo.out`, `power3.out`, and `sine.out`
- Primary transition: medium-energy focus pull, 0.5 seconds
- Accent transition: short vertical push between simulator and physical-device chapters
- No ambient zoom on the actual recordings; footage must remain readable

## What NOT to Do

- No gradients, neon glows, glossy device mockups, or fake reflections
- No decorative chat bubbles or fabricated UI
- No overlaid claims that are not visible in the source recordings
- No aggressive glitch effects or rapid cuts that obscure the workflow
- No background music or synthetic narration in this cut
